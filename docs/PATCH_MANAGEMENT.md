# Patch management - NIST SP 800-40 Rev. 4

Inventory dependencies/platforms; monitor vendor/NVD advisories; assess exposure and exploitability; prioritize; test build, migrations, security and workflows; approve; back up; deploy in stages; verify; monitor; and document rollback.

Emergency patches may shorten testing only with System/Risk Owner approval and compensating monitoring. Deferred patches require owner, reason, expiry and mitigations. FIPS-validated cryptographic requirements must be rechecked after crypto/runtime changes.
