# Security governance

## Required accountable roles

The deploying institution names: System Owner, Data Owner, Risk Owner, Incident Commander, Privacy Lead, AI Model Owner, Security/Vulnerability Owner, Backup/Recovery Owner, Integration Owner and third-party relationship owner. Technical Administrator access does not confer these governance authorities.

## Decisions

- Risk acceptance: documented risk, assets, impact/likelihood, compensating controls, owner, expiry and review.
- Security exception: control, business need, scope, evidence, owner, expiry and monitoring; no permanent exception without reapproval.
- Model change: dataset/code/artefact hashes, evaluation, privacy/security review, owner approval and rollback.
- Third party: risk class, data shared, location, security/privacy terms, incident/exit duties and annual review.
- Human oversight: AI cannot route, close or perform irreversible action.

## Review schedule

Monthly vulnerability/dependency/failed-control review; quarterly access, audit-chain, integration, retention, backup-restore and AI monitoring review; annual threat model, PIA, CSF profile, DR exercise, provider and independent security review; event-driven review after material incident/change.

Vulnerability disclosure, retention, supply chain, patching, incident response and recovery are governed by the corresponding repository policies.
