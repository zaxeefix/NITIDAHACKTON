# NIST alignment statement

Triage247Ng is designed to align with selected NIST cybersecurity, incident-response, identity, privacy, secure-development and AI-risk-management guidance. Final compliance depends on deployment configuration, institutional procedures, independent assessment and continuing monitoring.

NIST alignment does not replace the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, NDPC requirements or institutional policies. Triage247Ng is not NIST-certified, NIST-approved, fully NIST-compliant, or endorsed by NIST.

## Scope

The target profile uses NIST CSF 2.0 across Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover. Supporting references are SP 800-61 Rev. 3, SP 800-53 Rev. 5 Release 5.2.0, SP 800-63-4, SP 800-207, SP 800-218, SP 800-92, SP 800-122, SP 800-30 Rev. 1, SP 800-37 Rev. 2, AI RMF 1.0, Privacy Framework 1.0, SP 800-40 Rev. 4, SP 800-115, FIPS 199, FIPS 200 and FIPS 140-3.

Technical evidence is mapped in `docs/NIST_CONTROL_MATRIX.md`. Deployment and institutional responsibilities remain explicit; absence of source-code evidence is never converted into an implementation claim.
