# Privacy impact assessment

## Purpose and necessity

Triage247Ng collects information needed to receive, triage, investigate and route cyber incidents. Contact information is optional unless an institution requires follow-up. Reports must not request passwords, tokens, private keys, PINs or unrelated personal records.

## Personal data and risks

Email, telephone, institutional/numeric identifiers, free-text reports, screenshots, documents, device-local drafts and evidence may contain personal data. Risks include overcollection, OCR/redaction error, unauthorized original access, excessive retention, cross-border provider disclosure, insecure export and loss of an offline device.

## Controls

Data minimization; original/redacted comparison; editable redaction; formal approval; original-access permission and audit; evidence hashes/quarantine; role/resource checks; no API caching; legal-hold and retention fields; human disclosure approval; provider data-sharing description; and deletion/access/correction procedures.

## Required institutional decisions

The institution must document lawful basis, controller/processor roles, privacy notice, retention periods, data-subject intake and identity verification, breach notification, approved cross-border transfers, processor agreements, and NDPA/NDPC requirements. The application cannot make these legal decisions.

## Data subject workflow

1. Log an access, correction or deletion request with a request ID.
2. Verify requester identity through an approved channel.
3. Locate records without exposing unrelated incidents.
4. Place legal hold if required.
5. Obtain Data Owner approval.
6. Export, correct or securely delete eligible data/evidence.
7. Record scope, decision, outcome and evidence in the audit trail.
8. Notify the requester under the institution's required timeline.
